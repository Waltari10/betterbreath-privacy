# Privacy Policy for Lotus Breath

**Last updated: September 6, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how Lotus Breath ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our iOS application. By using the App, you consent to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the App.

## Summary

The App has **no user accounts and no sign-in**. Your breathing exercises, history, and progress live on your device and sync through your own private iCloud account, which we cannot access. Two kinds of data leave your device to us: **anonymous usage analytics, crash reports, and performance measurements**, which you can turn off at any time, and — only if you open the in-app store — a **record of any purchase**, which we need in order to give you what you paid for and to restore it on your other devices. Payment itself is handled entirely by Apple; we never see your card or billing details.

## Information We Collect

### Anonymous Analytics Data

We use **Mixpanel**, a third-party analytics service, to understand how the App is used and to improve it. We also use **Sentry**, a third-party error monitoring service, to detect and fix crashes and errors and to measure the App's performance.

When analytics is enabled, the following data is collected:

- **Anonymous identifier**: A randomly generated UUID created on, and stored on, your device. It is not linked to your name, email address, or any other personally identifiable information. If you delete and reinstall the App, a new identifier is generated and the previous one cannot be connected to it.
- **Approximate location**: Mixpanel derives an approximate location — city, region, and country — from the IP address your device connects from, and stores it alongside analytics events. This is coarse, city-level information. We do **not** collect GPS or precise location data, and the App never requests location permission.
- **Usage events**: Anonymous records of how the App is used, in these categories:
  - **App and screen usage** — app launches, moving to and from the background, which screens are viewed, and whether this install is a reinstall
  - **Onboarding** — progress through each onboarding step, which options were chosen, whether onboarding was skipped, and permission prompt outcomes
  - **Breathing sessions** — which exercise was started and from where in the App, its breathing pattern and length, pausing and resuming, completion, and how far a session progressed before being ended early
  - **Exercise library** — selecting, filtering, sorting, favouriting, creating, editing, and deleting exercises, and which suggestions the App offered and whether you took them
  - **Progress and rewards** — levels reached, streaks extended or lost, streak freezes earned, bought, or spent, XP boosts activated, the celebration screens, and when you share your progress, including which app you shared it to
  - **Purchases** — which product was chosen, and whether the purchase completed, was cancelled, or failed (with a technical reason when it fails), along with restores and the XP boosts or streak freezes they granted. These events identify the product and the outcome only; they never include prices, payment details, Apple's transaction identifiers, or your Apple ID.
  - **Reminders, widget, and prompts** — whether a daily reminder is set and at what time, opening the App from a reminder or from the lock screen widget, and when the App asks you to rate it
  - **Settings** — which setting was changed and its new value
  - **Technical quality** — data sync outcomes and degraded conditions such as audio, vibration, or writing to Apple Health failing
- **Device and app context**: App version and build number, whether the build came from the App Store or TestFlight, device model and manufacturer, operating system version, screen dimensions, and your language and region setting, sent alongside the events above. Some of these are added automatically by the Mixpanel software.
- **Aggregate progress context**: Your current level, streak length, total number of completed sessions, how many exercises you have created, how many days it has been since you installed the App, whether you have allowed notifications, whether the lock screen widget is installed, and which App settings are enabled — so that usage can be understood in context. These are attached to your events and are also kept as a profile in Mixpanel, filed under the anonymous identifier described above.
- **Crash and error reports**: When the App encounters a crash or error, Sentry automatically collects the error type and stack trace, device model, operating system version, app version, an anonymous installation identifier generated by Sentry, and a short trail of the actions and screens that led up to the problem.
- **Performance measurements**: Sentry also samples a small share of app sessions — roughly one in ten — to measure things like launch time and responsiveness. Unlike crash reports, this is collected when nothing has gone wrong. It contains timing and device information, not the content of your sessions.

Event properties never include text you have typed. Exercises you create yourself are identified in analytics by a random identifier and their numeric breathing pattern, **not by the name you gave them**. Names are only included for the App's own built-in exercises.

We do **not** collect or send to any server:

- Your name, email address, or any contact information
- Payment details — card numbers, billing addresses, or your Apple ID credentials. Apple handles payment; these never reach us.
- Precise or GPS location data
- Health data (HealthKit data stays on your device and with Apple)
- Browsing history or data from other apps
- Screenshots, screen recordings, or view hierarchies
- Any advertising identifier (IDFA). We do not track you across apps or websites, and we do not use your data for advertising.

Mixpanel's automatic event tracking is disabled — apart from the device and app context described above, only the events listed here are sent. Sentry collects data when an error or crash occurs and when a session is sampled for performance measurement, and it is configured not to send your IP address.

### Analytics Is Enabled by Default

Anonymous analytics is **on by default**. You are shown the choice during onboarding, on the final setup step, where you can switch it off before you start using the App. You can also change it at any time afterwards in the App's **Settings** screen under "Privacy". Your choice is stored alongside your other settings and syncs through your private iCloud account, so switching it off applies on every device signed in to the same iCloud account.

We rely on legitimate interest rather than consent for this processing — see "Legal Basis for Processing" below — and you have an unconditional right to object by switching it off.

### Opting Out of Analytics

When you turn anonymous analytics off:

- No further analytics events are sent
- Previously collected data remains anonymous and cannot be linked to you

Crash reporting and performance measurement via Sentry remain active regardless of the analytics setting, as they are necessary to keep the App stable and usable. This data is minimal and anonymous, and it is not used to build a picture of how you personally use the App.

### Data Stored on Your Device and in iCloud

The App stores your breathing exercises, practice history, settings, streak data, and anything an in-app purchase has granted you (an active XP boost and your streak freeze balance) locally on your device using Apple's on-device database. This data is automatically synced across your devices through your private iCloud account via Apple's CloudKit service. The synced data is stored in your personal iCloud storage and is not accessible to us. Apple's iCloud terms and privacy policy govern this data.

The App has no user accounts and no sign-in. Aside from the analytics, crash reporting, and purchase verification services described in this policy, we do not operate servers that hold your data, and we have no access to your iCloud data.

A small amount of additional information is kept in Apple's iCloud key-value storage rather than in the database: whether the App has been installed on your iCloud account before, and the purchase identifier and applied-purchase list described under "In-App Purchases". This storage belongs to your iCloud account and we cannot read it.

If you enable **Sync to Apple Health**, each completed breathing session is written to Apple HealthKit on your device as a mindful session, so it counts towards your mindfulness minutes. The App only ever writes this data — it does not request permission to read anything from Health, and we never see it.

### Notifications

If you enable the daily reminder, the App schedules a local notification on your device at the time you choose. The reminder text is fixed and generated on your device. Local notifications are handled entirely by iOS — no reminder content is sent through us or any third party.

We do not send you push notifications and we run no service capable of doing so. The App does permit the silent background notifications that Apple's iCloud sync uses to signal that your data has changed on another device. These are sent by Apple, carry no content, and are never shown to you.

### In-App Purchases (Apple StoreKit)

The App offers optional in-app purchases: **XP boosts**, which multiply the experience you earn for a limited period, and **streak freezes**, which protect your streak on a missed day. Nothing in the App is locked behind a purchase.

All payment processing is handled entirely by **Apple** through the App Store. We do not collect, process, or store any payment information such as card numbers, billing addresses, or Apple ID credentials, and we never learn who bought what.

#### What we receive

Apple issues a cryptographically signed receipt for each transaction. The App sends that signed receipt to our purchase verification service so that we can confirm it is genuine and work out what it entitles you to. We verify signatures on our own server rather than trusting the device, because otherwise a modified copy of the App could grant itself unlimited boosts.

Along with the receipt, the App sends a **purchase identifier**: a random UUID generated on your device. It is stored in Apple's iCloud key-value storage so that it survives reinstalling the App and follows your other devices, which is what makes restoring a purchase possible. It is not derived from your Apple ID, is not linked to your name or email address, and is never included in analytics events or crash reports.

The App contacts this service when you **open the store screen**, not only when you buy something: it fetches the current list of products and any entitlements already on record. That request carries your purchase identifier but no transaction, so nothing is recorded. The identifier is created at that point if it does not already exist, which means it can come into being before you have bought anything.

We store a record of each verified purchase containing:

- The purchase identifier described above
- Apple's transaction identifiers for the purchase
- The product identifier (which product was bought) and the quantity
- The purchase date
- The App's bundle identifier and whether the purchase was made in Apple's production or sandbox environment
- A refund date and reason, where Apple's receipt reports that the purchase was refunded
- The complete signed transaction record issued by Apple, retained for refund handling and customer support. This record is produced by Apple and also contains fields such as the App Store storefront your purchase was made in, and the price and currency Apple charged.

From these records we calculate your current entitlements — the multiplier and expiry of any active XP boost, and the total number of streak freezes you have bought — and send them back to the App, which applies them to your progress.

As with any request to an internet service, our purchase verification service receives your device's IP address in order to respond. We do not store it: there is no IP address in your purchase record and our code never reads or logs one. Our hosting provider may retain it briefly in its own infrastructure logs, as described in their privacy policy.

This information is pseudonymous: it is tied to a random identifier rather than to your identity, and we have no way to connect it to a person. Because a purchase record is nonetheless personal data under GDPR, see the "Legal Basis for Processing" section below.

Apple's App Store terms and privacy policy govern the payment transaction itself. For more details, see [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## How We Use Your Data

- **Anonymous analytics data**: Used to understand which features are used, where users have difficulty, and what to improve.
- **Crash reports and performance measurements**: Used to improve the App's stability and responsiveness.
- **Purchase records**: Used to verify that a purchase is genuine, to deliver what you bought, to restore it on your other devices, and to handle refunds and support requests.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or selling to third parties.

## Legal Basis for Processing

We process data under the following legal bases:

- **Anonymous analytics, crash reporting, and performance measurement**: **Article 6(1)(f) GDPR** (legitimate interest), to improve the App's performance, stability, and usability. We have assessed that our legitimate interest in improving the App does not override users' fundamental rights and freedoms, particularly given the minimal, non-identifying, and non-commercial nature of the data collected. You can object to the analytics at any time by switching it off in Settings, which stops all further collection.
- **Purchase records**: **Article 6(1)(b) GDPR** (performance of a contract). When you buy an XP boost or a streak freeze, verifying the transaction and recording it is necessary to deliver what you paid for and to let you restore it later. We also rely on **Article 6(1)(c) GDPR** (legal obligation) where transaction records must be kept for tax and accounting purposes.

The App has no accounts and requires no registration, so we process no data for the purpose of identifying you.

## Data Retention

- **Anonymous analytics events** are retained by Mixpanel for **180 days**, after which they are automatically deleted.
- **Crash reports, error reports, and performance data** are retained by Sentry for **90 days**.
- **Purchase records** are kept for as long as the purchase can still be restored or refunded, and thereafter for as long as tax and accounting law requires us to retain transaction records.
- **On-device data** (exercises, history, settings, and progress) persists until you delete it within the App or uninstall the App. Data synced to iCloud persists in your iCloud account until you delete it from the App on a synced device or remove it through your iCloud account settings.

## Deleting Your Data

Because the App has no accounts, there is no account to delete. You can remove your data as follows:

- **Individual exercises and sessions**: Delete them directly in the App — swipe to delete in the exercise library or in History.
- **All app data**: Delete the App from your device. To also remove the copy synced to iCloud, delete it in your device's iCloud settings (Settings > Apple Account > iCloud > Manage Account Storage) or delete the data in the App before uninstalling.
- **Analytics data**: Email us at [valtteri.e.laine@gmail.com](mailto:valtteri.e.laine@gmail.com) with the subject "Data Deletion Request". We will remove the associated data from Mixpanel and Sentry within 30 days. Because this data is anonymous, we may need the anonymous identifier from your device to locate it.
- **Purchase records**: Email us at the same address and we will delete them within 30 days, except where we are required to retain transaction records for tax and accounting purposes. We will need the purchase identifier from your device to find them, and deleting these records means your purchases can no longer be restored.

You can also switch analytics off in Settings at any time to stop all future analytics collection.

## Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area, you have the right to:

- **Access** information about what data we process — this policy describes it in full
- **Request deletion** of your data, as described in "Deleting Your Data" above
- **Object to processing** by switching analytics off in the App's Settings screen
- **Data portability** — your exercises and history are stored in your own iCloud account; analytics data is anonymous and cannot be linked to you. You can request a copy of your purchase records by email, quoting the purchase identifier from your device.

## Children's Privacy
